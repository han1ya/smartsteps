Montessori guide / teacher

Your observation and knowledge go a long way toward shaping a child's personality and love for learning. If you think you can make a difference in a child's life, we'd love to hear from you.

We're interested in applicants who have:
  • 2+ years of experience working in a licensed Montessori / childcare center
  • Are at least 20 years of age
  • Can meet accreditation requirements for education and pass DFPS background checks
  • Have a High School diploma

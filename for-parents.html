Montessori enrichment for your child

For Parents

How does a Montessori education benefit your child? Research and experience both point the same way.

Children attending Montessori schools tend to be creative thinkers, competent, self-disciplined, socially well-adjusted, and happy. The curriculum is broader than many other programs — developing a child's motor, perceptual, cognitive, volitional, emotional, language, social, and moral capacities all at once, helping the child become a genuinely competent learner.

Creative thinking

By exploring what they can see and touch first, children build a strong foundation from which to launch their imagination — comfort with open-ended activity unlocks real creative potential.

Competence

Children in Montessori schools are often above grade level in basic skills, and exceptionally knowledgeable in a number of other areas as well.

Self-discipline

Children choose to work long and hard, treat materials and others with respect, and build patience and the ability to focus for long periods.

Social adjustment

Visitors often find Montessori classrooms friendly, empathetic, and cooperative — a cheerful community where children happily help each other.

Happiness

Most parents of children in a Montessori school comment on how much their children love going.

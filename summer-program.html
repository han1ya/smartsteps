Super Summer Program

If you're wondering what you can do to combine learning and summer fun, look no further — here's a look at what we get up to.

Alphabet play

Sandpaper letters build early literacy. Children match letters to objects around the yard, or go on a letter hunt like an egg hunt.

Favorite food day

Children learn about nutrition and where food comes from, seeing, touching, smelling, and helping cut fruits and vegetables.

Measurements

Learning about different units of measure, and different ways to measure solids and liquids.

How stuff works

A simple, hands-on look at how everyday things and machines work.

Needle art

Sewing builds manual dexterity and hand-eye coordination, and is a valuable practical life skill.

Puzzle & Lego day

Puzzles and Legos build logic, brain function, and rational thinking.

Grow a garden

Planting, watering, weeding, and picking — with child-sized tools — fosters care for the environment.

Clay work

Outdoor modeling clay work lets each child create, and later paint, their own masterpiece.

Sand & water play

Shells, figurines, and tools for digging, pouring, and splashing — hours of educational play.

Bubble day

Blowing bubbles in different shapes and colors with a bubble machine.

Sea exploration

Matching sea creature figurines and labels to cards, with tongs for transferring practice.

Basic woodworking

Even young children can learn to use tools safely, with appropriate precautions and supervision.
